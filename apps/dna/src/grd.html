Elevation gradients / GRD

The pseudo-volume of elevated components: a top-light linear gradient laid over the ABV layer background, reinforcing the spatial model together with SHD shadows.

Grammar

ABV/<PRM|ACC>/<state>/GRD
ABV/PRM/RGL/GRD — #3B3B3B 50% → #212121 100%

Rules

  • The direction is fixed:
    • the gradient runs top to bottom;
    • lighter on top (the light source is above the observer);
  • Gradients exist only for ABV and only for the PRM and ACC emphases: for DFT/POS/ATN/NEG elevated components are flat;
  • The HVR and FCS states share one gradient, PRS has its own (darker/denser);
  • The DSB state gets no gradient: disabled components carry no volume;
  • The gradient is a layer over the FCL background, not its replacement;;
