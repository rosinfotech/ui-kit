Functional typography / FTY

Ten text roles through which components receive their typeface. A role references the absolute TXT matrix; color comes from FCL by the same principle of layers and emphases.

Grammar

FTY/<role>
FTY/HD2 — heading 2: Roboto Flex Medium 24

Vertical rhythm

There are no dedicated rhythm tokens — it is defined by the FTY section layout and assembled from the SPC scale. The same rhythm is applied to all DNA pages.

These roles are applied to the DNA documentation itself DNA: page headings are HD1/HD2/HD3, text is PAR, captions are FTN. The role order and labels mirror the FTY section in Figma: Header 1–3, Paragraph, Paragraph positive / attention / negative, Quote, Quote with author name, Footnote. It also shows demo cases of a paragraph wrapping an image left and right — those are PAR usages, not separate roles.
HD1 is the only role set in Inter (Medium 500); the rest use Roboto Flex. The HD1, HD2, HD3 and PAR roles also exist as font variables; the rest are yet to be created.
A mismatch in the Figma style registry: the role is labeled QTE_AUT while the style itself is named FTY/QTE_OUT. The style should be renamed to FTY/QTE_AUT.
