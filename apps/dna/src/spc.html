Spacing / SPC

A unified distance scale: outer and inner spacing, auto-layout gaps, control heights. Seven numeric variables applied directly.

Scale

XXS 4 px · 0.25 rem
SXS 8 px · 0.5 rem
SZS 16 px · 1 rem
SZM 24 px · 1.5 rem
SZL 32 px · 2 rem
SXL 48 px · 3 rem
XXL 64 px · 4 rem
The design system base unit is 4. All spacings, sizes and coordinates are multiples of it; the SPC scale is its multiple steps. Allowed exceptions are line thicknesses (THK starts at 1) and font metrics from TXT/FTY;.

Usage

  • gap / itemSpacing and padding of auto-layout layers are bound to the scale variables;
  • Control heights come from the same scale:
    • SZS = 16 — small size;
    • SZM = 24 — medium size;
    • SZL = 32 — large size;
