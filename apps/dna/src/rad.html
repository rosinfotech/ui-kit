Radiuses / RAD

The corner radius scale. Every value is bound to its own variable (IDs 18:3866…18:3872 for steps XXS…XXL).

Scale

XXS 4 px · 0.25 rem
SXS 8 px · 0.5 rem
SZS 12 px · 0.75 rem
SZM 16 px · 1 rem
SZL 24 px · 1.5 rem
SXL 32 px · 2 rem
XXL 40 px · 2.5 rem

Usage

  • The component default radius is RAD/SZS = 12 px (variable 18:3868, also used by vitrine frames);;
  • As the component size grows, the radius follows the scale: SZS → SZM → SZL;;
  • Radii above SZL are for large surfaces:
    • cards;
    • modal windows;
    • banners;
Radius variable names are not exported through the available read channels — only binding IDs are known. When building the library in Figma, names follow the scale steps: RAD/XXS…RAD/XXL.
